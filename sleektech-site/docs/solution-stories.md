# Customer-facing solution demos

The homepage and /work share seven distinct miniature product experiences. Each starts in the finished product. There are no requirements documents, implementation plans, code editors, tests or deployment screens.

Each product has five six-second scenes and a three-second final hold. The tablet remains stationary. Product actions and content transitions use the shared pause-aware clock. Wheel gestures scroll the surrounding website; long product screens scroll programmatically within the demo. Reduced motion displays the completed product state.

| Solution | Customer experience |
| --- | --- |
| Websites | Browse Forma's home page, explore projects, open a project, fill an enquiry, receive confirmation. Warm editorial colors and architectural compositions. |
| CRM | Review the lead pipeline, open Northwind's customer record and activity, review its proposal, move it to Won, see the updated open pipeline and revenue. Violet sales workspace with distinct pipeline columns. |
| Enterprise systems | Review purchase requests, inspect a request and approval route, open a supplier quote, confirm the purchase order, review spending. Blue procurement workspace. |
| Analytics | See a payment update revenue, choose Enterprise from the segment filter, see filtered KPIs/charts, open a customer revenue breakdown, share the quarterly report. Violet, teal and amber charts. |
| Commerce | Inspect orders and customer details, watch reservation/packing/carrier checks progress, open the shipping label, review sales. Green fulfillment workspace. |
| Logistics | Inspect stock, open a transfer, view its pick list, follow reserve/pick/pack/dispatch progression, review the network. Amber inventory workspace. |
| AI | Ten-message conversation with typed AI replies, order lookup, grounded policy answers, customer confirmation, replacement creation and return instructions. The conversation remains on screen throughout, with contextual evidence and actions alongside it. |

All names, data and actions are illustrative. These are authored visual demonstrations, not live integrations.
